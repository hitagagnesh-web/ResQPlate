# ResQPlate — Predict Surplus. Rescue Meals. Reduce Waste.

ResQPlate is an AI-powered food rescue platform designed to predict surplus food, connect donors with nearby NGOs and volunteers, and coordinate timely food collection to reduce food waste and fight hunger.

Unlike traditional food donation platforms, ResQPlate aims to **predict surplus before it happens**, helping recipients prepare in advance and ensuring food reaches people who need it.

## Key Features

* **Future Food Radar:** Predicts potential surplus locations, quantities, and time windows.
* **Live Rescue Radar:** Displays available food and nearby rescue opportunities on an interactive map.
* **AI Food Analysis:** Assists with food classification and estimated servings.
* **Smart Matching:** Connects donors with suitable recipients based on distance, capacity, and urgency.
* **Rescue Alerts:** Highlights urgent listings and helps coordinate timely pickups.
* **Route Optimization:** Generates efficient routes for volunteers collecting food.
* **Food Safety Assessment:** Screens listing information to support food safety checks.
* **Digital Food Passport:** Tracks food from donation to delivery.
* **Impact Dashboard:** Tracks meals rescued, food saved, and estimated environmental impact.
* **Demo Mode:** Simulates the complete rescue workflow for demonstrations.

## Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
* **UI and Animations:** shadcn/ui, Framer Motion, Lucide React
* **Charts and Maps:** Recharts, React Leaflet
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express
* **Database:** PostgreSQL / Supabase
* **AI Integration:** AI APIs or a modular prediction engine
* **Deployment:** Vercel and compatible backend hosting

*The final technology stack depends on the actual project implementation.*

## How It Works

1. Predict potential surplus food locations.
2. Allow donors to list available food.
3. Analyze listing details and urgency.
4. Match food with suitable NGOs or recipients.
5. Assign volunteers and optimize pickup routes.
6. Track delivery and update impact statistics.
